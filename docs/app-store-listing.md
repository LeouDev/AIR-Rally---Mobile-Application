## Description (4000 characters max)
AIR/Rally is the fastest way to book a pickleball court and find people to
play with.

**Book real courts, instantly**
See real-time availability at verified venues near you and reserve a court in
a few taps — no phone calls, no waiting for a reply. Every venue on AIR/Rally
is verified, so what you see is what you get.

**Never play alone**
Join Open Play sessions other players have posted, or share your own game and
let people request to join. AIR/Rally Ranked tracks your results as you play
— your first 10 matches calibrate your tier, and every match after that
keeps it current.

**COURT/Side**
Share what's happening on your court — photos, updates, and shoutouts to the
people you played with — in a feed built for pickleball, not another
general-purpose social app.

**AIR/Rally Credits**
When a booking is cancelled through no fault of yours, you're compensated in
AIR/Rally Credits instead of waiting on a refund — automatically applied to
your next booking.

**Built for pickleball, not adapted from something else**
Court surfaces, indoor/outdoor detail, real venue photos, and booking rules
that actually match how pickleball courts get reserved — because generic
sports-booking apps don't get the details right.

Download AIR/Rally and book your next game today.
